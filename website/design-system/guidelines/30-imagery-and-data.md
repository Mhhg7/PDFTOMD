# Imagery, icons & data

## Icons

Outlined, 1.75px stroke at 24px, rounded caps and joins — **Lucide** or **Phosphor Regular**, one library per project, never mixed. Filled icons only inside a coloured circle or hexagon.

Sizes: 16 inline with text, 20 inside buttons, 24 default, 32 for feature rows, 48 in feature cards inside a circle or hexagon. Colour is `navy-800` on light grounds, `white` on navy, `orange-500` inside a white circle on navy. An icon that carries meaning on its own needs 3:1 against its ground — `orange-500` on `surface` does not qualify, so pair it with a label or set it in `navy-800`.

Keep one icon per concept across every surface: pill, capsule, flask, truck, warehouse, map-pin, shield-check for GDP, thermometer for cold chain, handshake for partners, hospital, stethoscope, chart-up.

## Photography

Real and documentary: Al-Qawsan's own warehouses, delivery fleet, cold-chain rooms, teams, pharmacists and hospital partners in Iraq. Light, clean, neutral-cool grade; natural light; professional attire; the age and gender mix of the actual team.

For hero use, lay a `navy-800` overlay at 60–80%, or `gradient-horizon`, under any text. Product shots are packshots on `surface` or `surface-alt` with a soft shadow, cropped 4:5 or 1:1 and consistent within a set.

Never: 3D white clip-art figures (as on the current onboarding cover), generic Western hospital stock, spilled pills, syringe close-ups, or anything that implies self-medication.

## Illustration

Flat and geometric, built from the hexagon and line modules. Two colours — `navy-800` and `orange-500` — plus neutrals. No gradients beyond the three the system defines, no soft 3D, no drop shadows on illustration.

## Charts

Series colour order, in this order, every time:

1. `navy-800` 2. `orange-500` 3. `indigo-600` 4. `gold-400` 5. `navy-300` 6. `orange-300` 7. `gray-400`

Gridlines are `border-default`. Axis labels are `caption` / `ar-caption` in `text-muted`. Always label the unit, always cite source and period in `caption`, always give a series a direct label where there is room rather than a legend. Numbers are tabular.

A chart's time axis stays left→right even in an RTL page, unless the chart is Arabic-only. Do not mirror it just because the page is mirrored.

For the Iraq map: unserved governorates in `navy-100`, served governorates in `navy-800`, the five hubs as `orange-500` pins.
