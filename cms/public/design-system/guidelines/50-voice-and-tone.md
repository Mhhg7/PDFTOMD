# Voice & tone

**Tagline (EN):** Your Trusted Partner in Medical & Scientific Solutions
**Tagline (AR):** شريكك الموثوق في الحلول الطبية والعلمية

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Precise — we cite numbers, dates and certifications | Vague superlatives: "the best ever" |
| Trustworthy — calm, factual, compliant | Alarmist or salesy |
| Growth-minded — forward-looking, partnership language | Boastful |
| Respectful — formal Modern Standard Arabic, clear professional English | Slang, Iraqi dialect in official copy, emoji in formal material |

Write each language as an original. A sentence that only makes sense once you know the other language's version has to be rewritten, not re-translated.

Say "we / نحن" for the company and "you / أنتم" for partners and staff.

Never promise a medical outcome. Refer to approved product information and Ministry of Health registration; if a claim cannot be sourced, it does not ship.

Preferred terms:

| English | Arabic |
|---|---|
| pharmaceutical distribution | توزيع الأدوية |
| healthcare solutions | حلول الرعاية الصحية |
| strategic partner | شريك استراتيجي |
| governorates | المحافظات |
| scientific office | مكتب علمي |

The five core values, used as they are written, in both languages: Commitment / الالتزام · Quality of Work / الجودة في العمل · Teamwork / العمل الجماعي · Continuous Development / التطوير المستمر · Responsibility / المسؤولية · Discipline / الانضباط.

They have visual consequences, which is why they belong here: commitment is consistent grids with nothing off-alignment; quality is generous whitespace, crisp type and real photography; teamwork is the connected hexagons; continuous development is the upward arrow in the arc; responsibility is clear, cited, accessible data; discipline is strict token use — no ad-hoc colour, no ad-hoc font.
