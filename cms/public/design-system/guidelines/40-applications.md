# Applications

The web scale lives in the tokens. Slides and print run on their own scales, in points, and are set out here.

## Presentations (16:9, 1920 × 1080)

| Element | English | Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| Cover title | Montserrat 800, 72pt | Cairo 800, 76pt |
| Slide title | Montserrat 700, 40pt | Cairo 700, 44pt |
| Overline | Inter 600 caps, 16pt, `text-link` | IBM Plex Sans Arabic 600, 18pt, no caps |
| Card title | Montserrat 700, 22pt | Cairo 700, 24pt |
| Body | Inter 400, 18pt — never below 16pt | IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400, 20pt |
| Stat number | Montserrat 800, 60pt | — (Western digits) |
| Footnote / source | Inter 400, 12pt, `text-muted` | IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400, 13pt |

Twelve-column grid, 80px outer margins, 24px gutters. The Arc mark sits top-right at 80–100px on every slide, in the same position.

**Cover** — `gradient-horizon` over a warehouse or team photograph under a navy overlay; title in `white` with one key word in `orange-500`; presenter and role bottom-left in `body-sm`; address and website with orange icons.

**Agenda** — a two-column grid of numbered cards alternating `gradient-depth` and `surface-accent`, the number in a white circle, bilingual (English top or leading, Arabic below or trailing).

**Content slide** — `surface` ground, overline in `text-link` above the title in `text-heading`, both at the reading start; Arc top-right; 12-column grid.

**Stats slide** — a row of four stat tiles alternating navy and orange, with one navy banner sentence beneath.

**Partners slide** — logos in white `radius-lg` tiles on navy, equal tile size, grid-aligned. Drop the mirrored reflections in the current deck.

**Closing** — `gradient-horizon`, a centred bilingual message, three orange icon circles (Website / Coverage / Compliance), contact details.

## Print (A4)

| Element | English | Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| Document title | Montserrat 700, 24pt | Cairo 700, 26pt |
| Heading 1 | Montserrat 700, 16pt | Cairo 700, 17pt |
| Heading 2 | Montserrat 600, 13pt | Cairo 600, 14pt |
| Body | Inter 400, 10.5 / 15pt | IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400, 11.5 / 20pt |
| Table text | Inter 400, 9pt | IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400, 10pt |
| Footer / legal | Inter 400, 7.5pt | IBM Plex Sans Arabic 400, 8.5pt |
| Signage | Montserrat 700, ≥72pt | Cairo 700, ≥80pt |

**Letterhead.** Logo top-right on bilingual letterhead, a 3px `gradient-arc` bar under the header, a 12mm navy footer band carrying address, phone, website and email in white `caption`. Margins 20mm.

**Business card, 85 × 55mm.** Front: white, logo, name in Montserrat 700 11pt, title in Inter 8pt `text-muted`, contacts. Back: navy with the hexagon pattern and the white Arc. The Arabic face mirrors the English one.

**Notebooks and stationery.** A5 and A6 spiral notebooks keep the approved inversion — a navy cover with an orange footer band, and an orange cover with a navy footer band. This inversion is for covers, packaging and merchandise only; never invert a UI. Inside pages carry the group hexagon lockup at the head, "Date / Subject — التاريخ / الموضوع" in `text-link`, a 3px gold rule, `border-strong` ruled lines and a navy watermark at `opacity-watermark`. Pens: white body, full-colour horizontal logo.

**Signage and office notices.** White ground, Arc top centre, message in Cairo 700 (Arabic) / Montserrat 700 (English) in `text-heading` at 72pt or more, an optional orange hexagon icon, and an 8mm `gradient-arc` bar at the foot. This replaces the yellow-wave template.

## Social media

Post 1080×1080, portrait 1080×1350, story 1080×1920. Logo safe zone `space-10`.

- **Announcement** — navy ground with the hexagon pattern, Arabic headline in Cairo 800 with one word in `orange-500`, Arc in the bottom corner.
- **Product or partner** — white ground, packshot, partner logo inside a hexagon frame, navy information band at the foot.
- **Stat or milestone** — orange ground, a large `white` numeral (it is at display size, so it clears the 24px bold floor), label in `on-accent`.

Arabic leads for Iraqi audiences. No emoji in official posts.

## Email signature

Name in Montserrat 700 14px `text-heading`, role in Inter 12px `text-muted`, then phone · email · website in Inter 12px. Arc at 80px, above a 2px `gradient-arc` rule. No images of text, no quotations, no marketing banners.
